Subject: [RFC PATCH 0/9] slimbus: qcom-ngd-ctrl: propagate startup resume errors

The final patch returns power-up errors from runtime resume and checks both
resume paths before controller registration. Failed initial wake releases its
new QMI client while retaining the bootstrap PM reference; failed registered wake
retains existing registration/client ownership. Nonnegative PM success is accepted.
Eight prior bookkeeping, parent/child lifetime and DMA setup fixes precede it.
